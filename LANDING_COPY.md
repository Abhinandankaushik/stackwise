## FAQ
**1. How do you know what I should be paying?**
We maintain a pricing table sourced from every vendor's official pricing page, verified weekly. Every recommendation cites the URL. See PRICING_DATA.md in the repo.

**2. Is my data sent anywhere?**
Your spend inputs stay in your browser. Only if you opt in to the email report do we store your email + tools — company-identifying details are never on the public share link.

**3. Why is this free?**
Stackwise is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. If the audit surfaces real overspend, Credex can sometimes capture the rest. If it doesn't, you got a free audit. No catch.

**4. Will it tell me to drop tools I love?**
No. It tells you when you're on a plan that's wrong for your team size or use case. Recommendations are about right-sizing, not loyalty.

**5. Can I embed this on my blog?**
The embeddable widget is on the roadmap (week 2). For now, share the public audit URL — it has Open Graph previews built in.
